## Licensing boundaries
- Not used: official tournament name as product title, logo, trophy design, mascot, federation crests, real player names/likenesses, sponsor marks.
- Used as plain facts (nominative): country, city, stadium names, dates, draw groups.
- Stadium names that include a sponsor (e.g. «ملعب أرامكو») are the venue's public name; any promotional tie-in needs that partner's approval.
